NIFTY 50 scalping desk

Parameter search with a held-out test

Every combination of RSI trigger, stop and reward:risk is scored on the training days only. The best few are then shown on the test days they never saw. If test results collapse, the training result was overfit.

Method and notes

  • Signals: long when close is above intraday VWAP and RSI crosses up through the buy level; short is the mirror image. New trades only 09:30 to 14:45; everything is closed by 15:15.
  • No look-ahead: orders fill at the next bar’s open. VWAP and RSI both restart each day, so overnight gaps don’t distort them.
  • Realistic fills: if a bar opens beyond the stop, the fill is at the open, not the stop. If stop and target are both hit in one bar, the stop is assumed first.
  • Costs: brokerage (lower of ₹20 or 0.03%), STT 0.025% on sells, exchange 0.00297%, SEBI ₹10 per crore, stamp 0.003% on buys, 18% GST, plus slippage. Gross P&L is shown before slippage and charges.
  • Trained model: a logistic regression learns from training-day trades using RSI level, RSI slope, distance from VWAP, time of day and side. It only takes trades whose predicted win chance beats the training win rate.
  • Limits: each stock trades independently at the per-trade size, so total exposure can exceed capital; no portfolio cap is applied. Sharpe uses daily returns and is noisy with few days. Educational use only, not financial advice.
